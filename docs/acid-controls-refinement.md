# Acid floor visuals and inline support cooldown

Remove the unnecessary poison splash crown and its baked sprite factory/test. Acid now draws a wider terrain-following dark green pool, a saturated liquid interior, a thick neon-green edge and repeating filled bubbles/highlights. It stays visible at least three seconds on a miss and follows the longer poison-feedback duration when targets are affected. No flying impact droplets or splash sprite instances are created. Pool count, timed fading, reduced motion, attached drips and poison damage behavior remain intact.

Heal and Shield stay visible alongside the weapon choices, rather than opening a popover. A lock badge shows 3 → 2 → 1 for that player's own valid firing actions; a volley counts once. After the third shot, the action becomes usable on the player's next aiming turn. READY, NEXT and FULL distinguish available, waiting-for-turn and capped states. Choosing one ability consumes the shared unlock and starts a fresh three-shot cooldown, even if the unlock was held for extra turns. Full/invalid choices cannot waste it. AI and human support use the same game function. Heal remains +20 capped at 100; shield remains capped at 20.

The wheel grows from 92/80/72 to 108/96/84 pixels across desktop/tablet/phone sizes, retaining the half-outside-the-bar position. The existing reserved battlefield viewport band prevents ships from drawing behind it. The support controls wrap with the weapon group, using six compact slots in two rows on small phones. Original weapon JPEG icons remain unchanged.

Validation: 72 automated tests, TypeScript and production build pass. Countdown tests cover per-player progression, same-volley counting, next-turn gating, reset and a fresh cooldown after using a held unlock. Renderer tests verify pool lifetime/reset and that acid does not allocate splash sprites. Browser visual verification remains outstanding; the cloud browser cannot reach the workspace server. Large original music/loading assets use local test stand-ins only, excluded from this commit. Audio and weapon damage are unchanged.
